Policy overview

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored and shared when you visit this website or submit an enquiry through a website form.

By using the website, you acknowledge the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree with these practices, you may discontinue use of the website.

Independent aggregator

is an independent home security service aggregator. Service availability, provider terms, pricing and installation conditions may vary according to location and the selected provider.

Information we may collect

Information may be provided directly by you or generated automatically as part of normal website operation.

  • Your name when you submit an enquiry.
  • Your email address so the enquiry can be reviewed or answered.
  • The home security service category you select.
  • Information you voluntarily include in the contact form message.
  • Basic technical information such as browser type, device type and general website activity.
  • Browser storage information required for basic website preferences.

Please avoid submitting sensitive or unnecessary personal information through the contact form.

How information may be used

Information may be processed for legitimate website operation and service-related purposes.

  • To receive and process home security enquiries.
  • To respond to questions about available service categories.
  • To maintain website reliability, functionality and security.
  • To understand how visitors interact with website pages and improve usability.
  • To prevent spam, abuse, fraudulent form submissions or harmful website activity.
  • To comply with legal obligations where applicable.

When information may be shared

Personal information is not intended to be sold as a standalone product.

Information may be shared with technical partners or independent service providers where reasonably necessary to operate the website, process an enquiry or provide the assistance requested by the user.

Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law, regulation, court order or another valid legal process.

Service enquiries

If an enquiry needs to be reviewed by an independent security service provider, relevant information may be shared for that purpose. An independent provider's own privacy practices may differ from this website's policy.

Cookies and browser storage

The website may use essential browser storage required for normal functionality and basic preferences.

For example, local storage may remember that the compact cookie notice has already been accepted so it does not repeatedly interfere with navigation.

More information is available in our Cookie Policy .

Information retention

Information should only be retained for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected.

This may include time needed to answer an enquiry, maintain appropriate records, resolve disputes, prevent abuse or satisfy applicable legal obligations.

Retention periods may differ depending on the type of information and the reason it is processed.

Your privacy choices

Depending on your location and applicable privacy law, you may have certain rights concerning information connected with you.

  • Request information about personal data held about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Request deletion where applicable.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing where applicable.
  • Withdraw consent where processing relies on consent.

Privacy-related requests can be sent using the contact details provided below.

No internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Users should therefore avoid submitting sensitive information that is not necessary for a home security enquiry.
