Policy overview

This Cookie Policy explains how browser storage technologies may be used when you visit the website.

The website is designed to operate with limited storage and without unnecessary advertising or profiling technologies.

Minimal by design

Essential website storage is used only where it supports normal functionality, security or a basic user preference.

Cookies and browser storage

Cookies are small pieces of information that websites may store through a user's browser.

Similar technologies, such as local storage, can remember limited information between visits without requiring a traditional cookie.

These technologies may be used to remember basic preferences, support website security or prevent a notice from repeatedly appearing.

What browser storage cannot do

Normal website storage does not give us direct access to your personal files, photographs, documents or other private content stored on your device.

Essential website storage

Limited storage may be used where it is reasonably necessary for basic website functionality.

  • Remembering that the cookie notice has already been accepted.
  • Maintaining basic website preferences.
  • Supporting technical security where browser storage is required.
  • Helping prevent repeated or abusive website interactions where applicable.

Blocking all browser storage may cause some website preferences to reset between visits.

Analytics and measurement

Website analytics or measurement tools may be introduced where useful for understanding general performance, page usage or usability.

If a future analytics technology requires consent under applicable law, it should not be activated before the relevant consent mechanism and policy information are updated.

The current website does not require advertising profiling in order to provide its core content and enquiry functionality.

Third-party technologies

Some website functionality may rely on independent technical services, hosting infrastructure, font delivery or other external resources.

External services may maintain their own privacy and browser storage practices.

When you leave this website or interact with a third-party service, its own policies may apply.

Your browser controls

Most modern browsers allow users to view, clear or restrict cookies and local website storage.

  • Clear stored website data through browser privacy settings.
  • Restrict some or all website storage.
  • Use private browsing modes where appropriate.
  • Apply different storage permissions to individual websites where supported.

The exact location and wording of these settings depends on the browser and device you use.

The compact cookie notice

A small horizontal notice may appear near the bottom of the website when you first visit.

Selecting the acceptance option stores a simple local preference so the notice does not continue appearing on every page.

Clearing your browser's local website data may cause the notice to appear again.
